ABOUT THE EXPERIENCE

THE FUTURE IS AGENTIC

Agentic AI Day 2026 is a celebration of intelligent, autonomous and human-centric technology. It brings students, faculty and AI enthusiasts together to explore how AI can move from simply answering questions to reasoning, planning and taking action.

🤖

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI goes beyond traditional AI systems. AI agents can understand a goal, reason through a problem, plan the next steps, use tools and take actions to achieve an objective with greater autonomy.

✨

Why Agentic AI Day?

This event gives students a hands-on space to experiment with Generative AI, AI agents, prompt engineering, intelligent workflows, coding and creative AI. The goal is to turn curiosity into real-world innovation.

🔎

AI, Google & The Next Generation

Modern AI is rapidly changing search, software, creativity and productivity. Technologies from the wider AI ecosystem, including Google's AI innovations and developer tools, are helping people build smarter and more capable solutions.

🏫

Hosted by Vignan's

Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research brings together technology, learning and innovation through ACSE. Agentic AI Day is designed to encourage students to become AI-native engineers who build technology for meaningful human impact.
